What's inside

Everything you use. Nothing you don't.

Drift runs on Microsoft Edge's web engine, so sites work like they should. Everything around the page is Drift's own: small, quick, and out of your way.

Tabs down the side

Long titles stay readable. Drag to reorder, collapse with Ctrl B, and flip between recent tabs with Ctrl Tab, just like Alt Tab.

Ads blocked, built in

Uses the same filter lists as uBlock Origin: EasyList, EasyPrivacy, annoyances and malware lists. No extension to install.

One box for everything

Ctrl L searches the web, your history and your open tabs at once. Your home page and search engine are yours to pick.

Polite prompts

When a site asks for your camera, location or notifications, a slim bar slides in above the page instead of a pop-up in your face.

Downloads you can see

A progress ring in the toolbar and a clear list with pause, resume and "show in folder". Ctrl J opens it.

Updates itself

New versions download quietly and install the next time you close Drift. Your tabs come back exactly where you left them.

Privacy

Your browsing stays on your PC.

Drift has no account, no sign-in and no analytics. There's no Drift server that could collect anything.

Pages are shown by Microsoft Edge WebView2, which is part of Windows. Microsoft may collect diagnostic data from it according to your Windows privacy settings (Settings → Privacy & security → Diagnostics & feedback).

  • History, tabs and settings are localThey're saved in %AppData%\Drift and never uploaded. Clear them from History, or delete the folder.
  • No tracking, no telemetryDrift doesn't count you, profile you or phone home about what you do.
  • Only two things go online on their ownDrift asks GitHub whether a new version is out (a few times a day), and downloads the ad-blocking lists every 4 days. Neither request includes anything about you or your browsing.
  • Trackers blocked by defaultEasyPrivacy and uBlock's privacy lists stop most tracking scripts before they load.
  • Sites ask before they get anythingCamera, microphone, location and notifications are off until you allow them, and you can take that back in Settings → Site permissions.

FAQ

Questions

Is Drift free?

Yes. No ads, no paid tier, no account.

Will websites work?

Yes. Drift uses Microsoft Edge's engine (Chromium), the same one Edge and Chrome are built on, so modern sites, video and web apps work normally. If the ad blocker breaks a site, click the shield and turn it off for that site.

How do updates work?

Drift checks this project's GitHub releases when it starts and every few hours. A new version downloads in the background, is checked against the SHA-256 checksum GitHub publishes, and installs when you close Drift, or right away if you click "Update" in the toolbar.

Can I use Drift without installing it?

Yes. Download the portable .zip, unzip it anywhere and open Drift.exe. It updates itself in place, too.

Can I import my bookmarks or passwords?

Not yet. Drift doesn't have bookmarks or a password manager. Your sign-ins stay saved by the sites themselves.

Is the source code available?

No. Drift is distributed as a free download only.

How do I uninstall it?

Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Drift → Uninstall. You can choose to keep or delete your history and settings.

Give your tabs some room.

Drift is free, small, and installs in a few seconds.
